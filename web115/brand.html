Brand Guide

Brand Concept

Diana Digital is a personal technology brand focused on combining creativity, web development, and data analysis to create clean and useful digital experiences. The goal is to keep developing technical skills while creating work that is organized, approachable, and visually appealing.

Tagline

"Turning ideas and data into digital experiences."

Color Scheme

Soft Cream — #f7f3ee

Used for: page backgrounds

Dark Gray — #2f2f2f

Used for: body text

Deep Purple — #3a3153

Used for: headings and important text

Muted Purple — #5a3d7a

Used for: links and accents

Medium Gray — #777777

Used for: borders and secondary details

Font Choices

  • Arial — used for body text and general content.
  • Helvetica — used as a backup sans-serif font.
